Keys

Player 1
A D move, W jump, S crouch
J 1, K 2, U 3, I 4, L grab
O special 1, H special 2, Y super
Player 2
← → ↑ ↓ move, jump, crouch
; 1, ' 2, . 3, / 4, Enter grab
[ special 1, ] special 2, \ super
Dash
Double-tap left or right

Online, you can use either set of keys on your own machine: the host is Player 1 on the left, the guest is Player 2 on the right. Against the CPU you can use either set of keys. P pauses, Esc opens the menu. 1 and 2 are punches, 3 and 4 are kicks.

Moves

1, 2
Jab into straight
1, 2, 3
String that ends in a launcher
3, 4
Low-mid kick into high roundhouse
Down + 2
Uppercut launcher, then juggle
Down + 3
Low sweep, blocked only while crouching
Grab
Beats block, whiffs against jumps, slow if missed
Hold back
Block. Back + down blocks lows.

Hits land where the fist or foot actually is. High attacks pass over a crouching opponent.
